English for Future Entrepreneurs in Engineering

An advanced communication course preparing engineering undergraduates for high-stakes professional, entrepreneurial, and academic research settings. The third course in the ISE language sequence, building directly upon Professional Communication and Technical Writing.

CEFR Target: C1 (IELTS ~6.5 Entry) Format: 14 Weeks × 2.5 Contact Hours ABET Outcomes 3, 4, 5 SDGs 8, 9, 12

Target Multidisciplinary Cohort

Designed specifically for mixed teams from:

  • Aerospace Engineering (AERO)
  • Automotive Design & Manufacturing (ADME)
  • Information & Communication (ICE)
  • Nano-Engineering (NANO)
  • Robotics and AI Engineering

Pedagogical Framework

Constructivist 5E Instructional Model

Every weekly 150-minute block cycles through Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, and Evaluate, grounding language acquisition in unscripted simulation and authentic task delivery.

Dual-Translation Model

Research Rigor meets Venture Viability

Prepares students equally for international postgraduate fellowship applications (e.g., Fulbright STEM) and competitive commercial innovation pitches (e.g., Dyson Award).
