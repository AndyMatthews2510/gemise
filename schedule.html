14-Week Instructional Roadmap

Each weekly session is structured as a 150-minute active learning studio (3 hours including breaks), structured around the constructivist 5E instructional framework.

Weekly Schedule & Learning Phases

Wk Module Core Theme & Class Activities Primary Deliverables / Target
01 M1: Self & Career Enterprise Mindset & Tech Lexicon: Cross-major tech translation challenge; diagnostic pitch. SDG Problem Selection
02 M1: Self & Career Showcasing Strengths: Translating lab projects into impact narratives; drafting professional bio. Value Proposition Draft
03 M1: Self & Career Advanced Interviews: STAR-L framework; handling multi-part technical failure questions. Mock Triad Scrimmage
04 M1: Self & Career Simulation Lab 1: Live Mock Interviews: Multi-panel rotations; candidate & panel scoring. Assessment 1 (20%)
05 M2: Negotiation Engineering Meetings: Agenda control, diplomatic interruption, writing RACI action items. Meeting Minutes & Agenda
06 M2: Negotiation Principled Negotiation: BATNA, ZOPA, conditional bargaining syntax ("if/then" concessions). Bilateral Licensing Drill
07 M2: Negotiation Multi-Party Disputes: Stakeholder mapping, resolving ethical safety dilemmas (ABET 4). 4-Way Stalemate Scrimmage
08 M2: Negotiation Simulation Lab 2: Crisis Negotiation: Live 4-way multi-party simulation & joint resolution. Assessment 2 (25%) + MoU

10 M3: Proposals Feasibility & Budgets: Bill of Materials (BOM) construction, Failure Modes (FMEA) risk analysis. Red Team Stress Test
11 M3: Proposals Drafting Studio & Calibrated Peer Review: Technical editing, visual data integration. Assessment 3 (25%) Due
12 M4: Pitching Pitch Decks & Defensive Rhetoric: Slide minimalism, handling hostile investor Q&A. Rapid-Fire Q&A Scrimmage
13 M4: Pitching Capstone Pitch Defense (Round 1): Team pitches, panel Q&A, mock investment allocation. Assessment 4 Part 1
14 M4: Pitching Capstone Pitch Defense (Round 2) & Synthesis: Final pitches, funding awards, course debrief. Assessment 4 Part 2 (20%)

The Weekly 150-Minute Class Engine

  1. Engage (15 min): Real-world technical hook, video analysis, or incident breakdown.
  2. Explore (40 min): Collaborative sandbox task in cross-disciplinary teams.
  3. Explain (30 min): Instructor micro-lecture on C1 discourse strategies & syntax.
  4. Elaborate (50 min): High-stakes simulation execution or collaborative drafting studio.
  5. Evaluate (15 min): Rubric-referenced feedback, peer debrief, and milestone checkpoints.

Google Classroom Workflow

Assignments operate on a scaffolded submit-and-revise workflow. Checkpoints in Weeks 2, 5, 9, and 10 provide formative feedback before high-stakes summative evaluations in Weeks 4, 8, 11, and 13–14.
