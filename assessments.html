Assessment Rubrics & Evaluation Instruments

Analytical scoring criteria aligned with CEFR C1 language benchmarks and ABET Student Outcomes (3, 4, 5). Formatted for direct upload to Google Classroom or physical printouts.

Assessment 1: Mock Technical & Behavioral Interview (20%)

Individual Evaluation • Target: CEFR C1 Spoken Production • ABET Outcomes 3 & 4

Criteria Exemplary (4 / C1) Proficient (3 / B2+) Developing (2 / B2) Remediation (1 / B1)
STAR-L Narrative Arc (25%) Structured logical flow; personal agency clear; mature reflection on failure/learning. Clear STAR structure; minor gaps in technical action or quantified result. Descriptive narrative; wanders into minor details without clear action. Disorganized; vague generalizations with no specific engineering incident.
Technical Lexicon (25%) Precise disciplinary vocabulary; translates concepts clearly to outside stakeholders. Accurate terminology; minor slips in word choice that do not obscure meaning. Repetitive, basic terms; struggles to translate jargon into clear English. Frequent vocabulary errors; informal register obscures technical meaning.
Pragmatic Poise & Fluency (25%) Executive presence; signposts multi-part answers; confident composure under pressure. Speaks fluently with natural pacing; handles direct follow-up questions well. Hesitant delivery; relies on filler phrases; visible strain during follow-up questions. Noticeable breakdown under pressure; fails to address follow-up questions.
Value Proposition (25%) Persuasively showcases technical strengths with concrete evidence of impact. Clearly identifies personal strengths and project accomplishments. Lists skills as a static inventory without connecting to real outcomes. Unable to articulate personal strengths or relate skills to the role.

Assessment 2: Meeting Facilitation & Negotiation Simulation (25%)

Team/Cluster Evaluation • Target: CEFR C1 Spoken Interaction • ABET Outcomes 3, 4, 5

Criteria Exemplary (4 / C1) Proficient (3 / B2+) Developing (2 / B2) Remediation (1 / B1)
Agenda Control & Leadership (25%) Manages discussion purposefully; gatekeeps rabbit holes; summarizes consensus. Follows agenda items; transitions smoothly; encourages team contributions. Struggles with time management; discussions wander; unresolved agenda items. Lacks direction; frequently derailed; no clear action items recorded.
Integrative Negotiation (25%) Identifies underlying interests; deploys conditional proposals; protects BATNA. Balances interests against concessions; reaches viable agreement without friction. Relies on positional bargaining; concedes too quickly or remains inflexible. Adversarial or passive; reaches impasse or accepts unfavorable terms.
Diplomatic Register (25%) Uses tactful reframing and professional hedging; de-escalates tense impasses. Professional meeting register; minor slips into blunt or overly casual phrasing. Overly blunt; struggles to state disagreements constructively. Inappropriate tone; confrontational or overly passive language disrupts session.
Joint MoU Synthesis (25%) Written MoU is precise, comprehensive, unambiguous, and covers risk allocation. Accurately documents key terms and compromises with minor formatting gaps. Vague summary; omits key technical tradeoffs reached during talks. Incomplete, contradictory, or inaccurate documentation of agreed terms.

Assessment 3: Proposal Dossier (25%)

Team Submission (2,500 words) • ABET 3 & 4

  • Executive Abstract & SDGs (20%): Problem severity quantified; SDG 9/12 metrics defined.
  • Technical Design (25%): Interdisciplinary architecture, CAD/schematics, V&V protocol.
  • Budget & FMEA Risk (25%): Bill of Materials, unit economics, complete RPN risk matrix.
  • C1 Technical Style (30%): IEEE citations, technical conciseness, visual data formatting.

Assessment 4: Pitch & Defense (20%)

Team Presentation + Individual Q&A • ABET 3 & 5

  • Narrative Arc & Hook (25%): 10-minute pitch linking problem, technology, and market value.
  • Visual Economy (20%): High-density graphics; minimal text; clear data architecture.
  • Defensive Q&A (35% - Indiv): Technical composure under hostile panel questioning.
  • Team Coordination (20%): Balanced speaking time; seamless interdisciplinary handoffs.
